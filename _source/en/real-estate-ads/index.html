Real estate advertising: Facebook, Instagram and Google

Facebook, Instagram and Google campaigns for brokers, with one landing page per campaign and tracking of every lead, managed monthly.

Home estimate requests, not just clicks.

Our seller campaigns invite homeowners in your areas to request an estimate of their home's value. Targeting follows your area, the message speaks to their situation, and every request goes straight into your CRM for an immediate reply. A click is worth nothing without follow-up: follow-up is what turns a request into an appointment.

Showcasing your listings

We also promote your listings to buyers in your market and build buyer campaigns that grow your contact list. A well-promoted listing also shows future sellers how you market a property.

One landing page per campaign

Each campaign leads to a clear page, on brand, that matches the ad exactly, with a single goal and a form connected to your CRM. You know which campaign brings in which requests.

Tracking and monthly reporting

We track every request from the ad to the CRM, and we adjust targeting, messages and budget based on the requests you actually receive. Every month, you receive a clear report. Results vary with the market, budget and offer, and we do not promise any cost per lead.

A clear budget, no long-term commitment

Together we set a starting ad budget based on your areas and goals, then adjust it after the first few weeks based on the requests you receive. Our management fees are set in writing in the proposal, separately from the ad budget. Campaigns can be paused during a slower period or restarted before a new listing.

Ads prepared around the rules of the profession

Your name as it appears on your licence, your title and your agency are clearly identified, with the agency displayed prominently. You approve every ad before it runs. The ad budget is paid directly to the platforms.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manage Facebook and Google ads for brokers?

Yes. We create and manage your Meta and Google campaigns every month, with lead tracking and a clear report. Results vary with the market, the budget and the offer.

Do you follow OACIQ advertising rules?

We include in your ads and website the mentions required by OACIQ advertising rules, such as your name as shown on your licence, your title and your agency. Outside Quebec, the rules of your own regulator apply. Compliance remains your responsibility and your agency's. We do not give legal advice: for a specific case, contact the OACIQ or your agency's executive officer.

Why a landing page for each campaign?

A page that matches the ad exactly, with a single form connected to your CRM, shows which campaign brings which requests and lets us adjust the budget based on the requests you actually receive.
