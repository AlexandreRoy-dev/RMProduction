FAQ, industrial marketing

Why a bilingual industrial website?

Your buyers, distributors and OEM customers are often outside Quebec. A technical site in French and English presents your capabilities to both markets and meets B2B buyer expectations.

What should a manufacturer's website include?

Clear product and capability pages (processes, equipment, materials, tolerances, certifications), past projects, and a quote request form that collects the right information from the start.

How do you qualify quote requests?

With a structured form (part type, quantities, materials, deadline, attached files) connected to your CRM, so your team receives complete, prioritized requests.

Do you build custom software for manufacturers?

Yes: client portals, dashboards, ERP or CRM integrations and automation of repetitive tasks, designed around your processes.

Do you run B2B advertising?

Yes, on Google and LinkedIn or Meta depending on your buyers, tracking quote requests received rather than clicks alone.
