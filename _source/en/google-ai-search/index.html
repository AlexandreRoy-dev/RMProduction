Sellers search differently now

Before calling a broker, a seller searches. They type a question into Google, check the map to see who works in their area, and read reviews. More and more, they also ask an AI assistant directly, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews: which broker should I choose to sell my house, how do I estimate my home's value, how long will the sale take. These tools answer with only a few names. They cite the brokers they understand well, based on the information they find online. If that information is incomplete or inconsistent, another name takes your place.

Google search (SEO)

It all starts with your website. We fix what holds Google back: speed, page structure, tags, and properly linked English and French versions. We build a page for each area you serve, written to answer the questions sellers in that area ask rather than to repeat keywords. Internal links connect these pages to your services, your home estimate request page and your CRM. Google Search Console is set up to track what Google sees, the searches that lead to you and the pages to improve.

Google Business Profile and the Map Pack

Your Google Business Profile is often a seller's first contact with you. Since most brokers serve an area rather than an office open to the public, we set it up as a service-area business with the address hidden, following Google's rules. Categories, services, areas, photos and posts are completed and kept up to date. A simple review process, triggered at the right moment by your CRM, helps you collect feedback from satisfied clients, with reply templates you approve. All of this supports your presence in the Map Pack.

Visibility in AI answers (GEO)

GEO, or generative engine optimization, means making your practice easy for AI assistants to understand and cite. The first step is a consistent entity: the same name, phone number, website and description everywhere, on your site, your Google Business Profile, directories and social media. We then add structured data to your site that clearly describes your practice, your agency, your services and your area, plus an llms.txt file that summarizes the essentials for AI tools. Finally, we write FAQs that answer the real questions sellers ask, with precise, citable content rather than vague text no one picks up.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee any position on Google or in AI answers. No one controls these results, and an agency that promises a ranking is misleading you. What we do control: the quality of your website, your Google Business Profile, your information and your content.

How we measure

We track progress with Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile insights, such as calls, direction requests and website visits. We also regularly test seller questions in the main AI assistants to see whether your name comes up. Every month, you receive a clear report on what was done, what moved and what comes next, with no inflated numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI search optimization, or GEO?

GEO (generative engine optimization) means making your practice easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand and cite. In practice: consistent information everywhere, structured data on your website, an llms.txt file and clear answers to the questions sellers ask.

Can you guarantee a top spot on Google or in ChatGPT?

No. Nobody controls Google results or AI answers, and an agency that guarantees a ranking is misleading you. We work on what influences those results: your website, your Google Business Profile, the consistency of your information and the quality of your content.

Can a broker have a Google Business Profile without showing an address?

Yes. If you serve clients in an area rather than at an office open to the public, Google allows a service area profile with the address hidden. We create or optimize it under Google's rules, with your areas, services, photos and a review process.

What do you actually do for my search visibility?

A starting audit, then: technical and content work on your website, area pages, your Google Business Profile and Map Pack presence, consistent name, phone and website across directories, structured data, llms.txt and FAQs written to answer the questions sellers really ask.

How do you measure progress?

With Google Search Console, your Google Business Profile insights (calls, direction requests, website visits) and regular tests of seller questions in the main AI assistants. You get a clear report every month, with no inflated numbers.
