A real estate agent website that brings in requests

Fast, elegant and bilingual, with your listings, neighbourhood pages and a home estimate request connected directly to your CRM.

Everything a seller looks for before calling you.

A seller often visits your website before picking up the phone. They want to see who they are trusting with their home, how you present your listings and whether you know their area. Your site brings together carefully presented listings, neighbourhood pages, a home estimate request page, your bio, your approach and your contact details, in English and in French.

Neighbourhood pages and listings

Each neighbourhood page presents the area, the types of homes and your read on the market. These pages help sellers find you on Google and in AI answers, and they are the base of your search visibility. Your listings are showcased with large photos, a clear description and a call to action that leads to you.

A home estimate request connected to your CRM

The home estimate request page is the heart of the site. The seller describes their property and asks you, their broker, for an estimate of its value. The request goes straight into your CRM and triggers a reply and a follow-up task. It is an estimate from you, their broker, not a report from a certified appraiser, and the form says so clearly.

Fast, bilingual, ready for Google and AI

Fast loading on mobile, a clear structure, tags and structured data in place, and linked English and French versions. The site is built from day one for Google and AI search visibility, so your name comes up when a seller looks for a broker.

Your agency, clearly identified

Your name as it appears on your licence, your title and your agency are built into the site template, with the agency displayed prominently. You approve every page before it goes live. Your domain name is registered in your name, and the site ownership terms are written into our proposal.

Frequently asked questions

What does a real estate agent website include?

A design that fits your brand, your listings, neighbourhood pages, a home estimate request page and forms connected directly to your CRM. The site is fast, bilingual on request and built for Google and AI assistants.

Does the website show the mentions required by the OACIQ?

Yes. Your name as shown on your licence, your title and your agency are built into the template. Compliance remains your responsibility and your agency's, and you approve every page before it goes live.

What happens when a seller fills out the form?

The request goes straight into your CRM, the seller gets an automatic reply and you are notified to call back. No request gets lost in an inbox.
