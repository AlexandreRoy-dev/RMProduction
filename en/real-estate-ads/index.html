Frequently asked questions

Do you manage Facebook and Google ads for brokers?

Yes. We create and manage your Meta and Google campaigns every month, with lead tracking and a clear report. Results vary with the market, the budget and the offer.

Do you follow OACIQ advertising rules?

We include in your ads and website the mentions required by OACIQ advertising rules, such as your name as shown on your licence, your title and your agency. Outside Quebec, the rules of your own regulator apply. Compliance remains your responsibility and your agency's. We do not give legal advice: for a specific case, contact the OACIQ or your agency's executive officer.

Why a landing page for each campaign?

A page that matches the ad exactly, with a single form connected to your CRM, shows which campaign brings which requests and lets us adjust the budget based on the requests you actually receive.
