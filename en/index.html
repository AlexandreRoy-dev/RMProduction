Listings are rarely lost for lack of talent.

They are lost because a call was not returned, a request sat in an inbox, or a seller found another broker online before finding you. The portals sell you leads you share with other brokers. We build assets in your name instead: a website, a presence in Google and in AI answers, a CRM and campaigns that work for you every day, even during your showings.

Real estate agent website

A fast, elegant, bilingual site with your listings, neighbourhood pages and a home estimate request page connected directly to your CRM.

Google and AI search visibility

When a seller asks Google or an AI which broker to choose, your name should come up. An optimized site, Google Business Profile, reviews and structured content for Google, the Map Pack and answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

CRM and automated follow-up

Whatever CRM you already use, we configure and automate it: instant replies, follow-ups, online booking and a clear pipeline.

Real estate advertising
