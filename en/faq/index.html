General questions

Do you work only with real estate brokers?

Yes. Roy Marketing is a real estate marketing firm: we work exclusively with brokers, teams and agencies.

Do you offer your services in French?

Yes. We work in French and English, and your websites, ads and content can be bilingual.

How does a project work?

Four steps: a consultation to understand your market and your tools, a written plan with the steps and prices, setup of the website, CRM and campaigns, then monthly follow-up based on the requests you actually receive.

How much do your services cost?

The price depends on your starting point and what you want to achieve. After the consultation, you receive a clear written proposal, free of jargon, with each step and its price.

Why do AI assistants recommend some brokers over others?

They rely on what they can find and understand online: a clear website, the same information everywhere, reviews and precise answers to sellers' questions. Our Google and AI search service works on each of these points.

Can my current website be optimized for Google and AI?

Often, yes. A starting audit shows what can be fixed on your current site and what needs a rebuild.

Do you name your clients in your case studies?

Only with their consent. Every case study we publish is real and approved by the clients involved.

Another question?

We are happy to answer it during a consultation, with no commitment.
