CRM FOR REALTORS

CRM and automated follow-up for real estate agents

Whatever CRM you already use, we configure it, connect it to your website and ads, and automate your follow-up.

Book a consultation

We work with every CRM.

If you already have a CRM, we start from what you have: your contacts, your habits, your stages. If you do not, we help you choose the one that fits your practice, your team and your budget, then we set it up. Our job is not to sell you software but to make sure no request gets lost.

Follow-up happens, even during showings.

A home estimate request comes in on a Sunday evening or during a showing: the seller gets an instant reply by text and email, and a task is created for you. Scheduled follow-ups continue with sellers and buyers who are not ready yet. Appointments are booked online, with automatic reminders. Every morning, a summary shows you what needs your attention.

Seller and buyer pipelines

Pipelines designed for real estate, from first contact to signed listing and on to the sale. Every prospect moves from one stage to the next, and next actions always stay visible. You see at a glance where each client stands.

Teams and agencies

For a team or an agency, the CRM is shared: lead assignment, pipelines per agent and reports for management. Everyone knows which requests are theirs and what to do next. New agents can be added and trained without rebuilding the system.

Setup and training

Configuration, import of your contacts, connections to your website, your home estimate request page and your ads, then training for you and your team. You approve every automated message before it is switched on, and your data stays in your CRM, in your name.

Frequently asked questions

Which CRM do you use for real estate brokers?

We work with every CRM. If you already have one, we set it up, connect it to your website and ads, and automate your follow-ups. If you don't, we help you choose and set up the right one for your practice.

What do you automate?

Instant replies by text and email, follow-up reminders, online booking and moving each lead from one stage to the next in your seller and buyer pipelines.

Do you train the broker or the team?

Yes. Setup includes training for you and your team, so everyone knows where to find their leads and what to do next.
